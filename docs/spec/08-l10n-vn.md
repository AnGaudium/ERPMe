# Vietnam localization: VAS rules

These rules decide whether the books comply with the standards, so they are designed together with the core, not later. The mechanism lives in the core; Vietnam-specific rules live in the Vietnam package. Items marked unverified must be checked against the original legal text before implementation.

## Costing

- **Default: periodic weighted average.** Scope per warehouse or company-wide, recorded in the accounting policy. VAS 02 requires consistency; a change of method is applied retrospectively under VAS 29. Moving average is a later option.
- **Preliminary price during the period:** issues carry the previous period's average price, used for simulation, below-cost warnings and margin. At period close the engine solves average prices across warehouses and BOM levels together (equation system or iteration to convergence), then posts COGS adjustments with a cost trace.
- **Transfers** move at the issuing warehouse's average price.
- (Phase 2) **Disassembly:** allocate value to components in proportion to average or standard price.
- (After Phase 1) **Standard cost:** secondary sources say TT99 introduced the standard-cost method and dropped accounts 611/631 (unverified). If true, offering standard cost early may help assembly customers.

## Imports and purchase costs

- The customs declaration is its own document, with the customs duty rate separate from the accounting rate.
- Import duty (and special consumption tax if any) is capitalized into cost. Import VAT posts to 33312 and 1331.
- Later purchase costs (freight, handling) are allocated by value, quantity or weight. If goods were partly issued, the corresponding share goes to COGS.

## E-invoicing and tax

- **Invoice errors** are handled by adjustment or replacement invoices, linked to reversal documents, returns, trade discounts (521) and promotional goods. Decree 70/2025 removed cancellation of erroneous invoices (unverified).
- **Invoice timing** is the transfer of ownership, so the approval queue must not delay issuance.
- **Adapter for several e-invoice providers** (BKAV, Viettel, VNPT, MISA…) behind one interface. Phase 1 builds one provider (BKAV); other providers are an easy community contribution.
- **Purchase invoices:** fetched through an authorized e-invoice provider or T-VAN, or through an invoice-receiving mailbox. Never rely on bypassing the lookup portal's captcha. Check invoice status (adjusted, replaced) and the seller's tax-ID status.
- **VAT deduction warning:** invoices of VND 5 million or more paid in cash do not qualify for input VAT deduction (VAT Law 48/2024, unverified).
- **Date-effective tax rates** (e.g. VAT reduced to 8%), and export of the 01/GTGT return XML for HTKK/eTax.

## Minimum processes to operate for real

- Cash: cash receipts, cash payments, cash book, cash count.
- Employee advances (141) and settlement.
- Expenses without a PO.
- General journal entries, with approval.
- Netting receivables and payables of the same partner. Payment discounts (515, 635).
- Credit limit including undelivered orders.
- Stock count differences (surplus, shortage), returns with lots and serials.
- Period-close checklist: bad-debt provision by aging bucket, inventory write-down provision, prepaid expense amortization (242), closing to 911 and 421.
- Opening balance import (see Data migration).
- Later: consignment.
