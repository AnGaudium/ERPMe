# Multi-UoM observations from the simulator author (read of design at 32e7b1c)
1. Purchase line unit precedence: ADR-0018 r12 product purchase_uom_id proposed; ADR-0024 r2 supplier item min/multiple "in the purchase unit" but DS-43 supplier_item has no unit column; partner_item.uom_id is a third candidate. Proposal: typed unit wins; default partner_item unit else product purchase_uom_id; supplier_item gets its own unit or states it uses purchase_uom_id.
2. product_uom is per product only (ADR-0018 r10): variants and configured items share factors; no per-item override (e.g. configured frame bought by length).
3. No conversion path: find_price returns uom_not_allowed (ADR-0045 r4); ADR-0018 r10-11 do not name the document-line error; company units (x-) convert only through product_uom (r8).
4. GR/IR price per inventory unit (ADR-0047 r10, r13) is consistent; not stated whether a receipt line in another unit converts with the order line's factor or its own unit.
5. Three-way match price tolerance (ADR-0047 r14) compares invoice unit price with GR/IR price per inventory unit: units differ when the invoice is in the supplier's unit; state per-inventory-unit comparison and whether price_tolerance_amount is per inventory or invoice unit.
6. Rounding: ADR-0018 r11 rounds inventory quantity half away from zero (quantity_rounded / quantity_rounds_to_zero), never changes amount (ADR-0006 r19); not stated whether GR/IR positions and layer price use the rounded or exact quantity; partial receipts each rounded summing to a different inventory quantity than the order line.
7. Receipt tolerance (ADR-0047 r11) on open quantity of the supply row (inventory unit): confirm for receipts entered in another unit.
8. Price breaks across units (ADR-0045 r4): entered unit's breaks first, fallback inventory unit; fallback x factor can be cheaper; entered unit always wins: needs a worked example.
9. Request -> order: planning, demand/supply rows and purchase requests are in inventory unit (ADR-0024 r6); not said whether a PO copied from a request keeps inventory unit or changes to purchase unit, or how supplier min/multiple apply; e-invoice line in supplier unit mapped to an order line in another unit: purchase-invoice agent D3 (ADR-0051 r21) has no unit rule.
