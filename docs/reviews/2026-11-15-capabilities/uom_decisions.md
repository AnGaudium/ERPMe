# Architect decisions for ADR-0056 Multiple units of measure (2026-11-15)
Sources: uom_research.md (D1-D20), uom_audit.md (U-01..U-29), uom_sim_observations.md (1-9). Author's direction: the conversion is defined on the item master data; multi-UoM must be complete in the design before testing.

Core
1. (D1) The inventory unit stays the only unit of stock quantity, value, costing layers, MRP netting, demand/supply rows and GR/IR positions. Every conversion happens in the command layer when a line is taken in (ADR-0018 r9 confirmed).
2. (D5, sim 2, author) Conversions live on the item master: product_uom keeps product-level factors and gains a nullable item_id for an item-level (variant / configured item) override. Resolution order: item row -> product row -> global uom_conversion (same quantity kind, direct pair or its inverse; no chaining through a third unit). One pure function convert_quantity(item, from_unit, to_unit) returns (factor n/d, source: item|product|global|identity, is_exact). Company units (x-) convert only through product_uom.
3. (D3, U-01) Each product unit is classified exact or inexact at creation: exact when numerator*10^dI is divisible by denominator*10^dE (dI, dE decimal places of the inventory and entered unit). For a line received/issued/invoiced in parts against a source line in the same entered unit, each part's inventory quantity = round(cumulative entered x factor) - round(previous cumulative entered x factor) (difference of rounded cumulative quantities, as GR/IR values do). A part in another unit converts on its own and closes by inventory-quantity comparison with tolerance. Invariant: parts in the source line's unit that sum to the source's entered quantity sum to its inventory quantity exactly.
4. (D2, U-04) Every quantity-bearing line (commercial, stock, production, transfer, count, BOM, planned order) stores: entered quantity, uom_id, conversion snapshot (numerator, denominator, factor source), inventory quantity. Never re-read from master data after the line exists.
5. (D8, U-07) A product_uom / uom_conversion row is immutable once any line references it; to change, retire it (is_active false; no new lines may use it) and add a new row. No effective dating. uom_conversion is immutable once used and has is_active too.
6. (D9-D10, U-21, U-23) Rounding by use case through one function round_quantity(q, unit, mode): requirements, proposals, issue multiples and supplier multiples round up; receipts, deliveries, invoices and counts round half away from zero; never changes amounts (ADR-0006 r19). Serial-tracked products: every product_uom factor to the inventory unit is a whole number. Scrap Q = G/(1-scrap) rounds up in the component's inventory unit.
7. (D4, U-02, sim 4, 7) Follow-on documents may use any allowed unit of the item (receive pieces against a carton order, invoice per kg). Links, open quantities, receipt tolerance, three-way match quantities and GR/IR compare inventory quantities. Receipt tolerance applies to the ordered inventory quantity (not the open quantity) (U-22). A return or credit line priced from its original converts the original's price per inventory unit by its own factor.
8. (D12, U-11, U-12, sim 5) One unit and one price per line; unit prices carry up to 10 decimals; no separate order-price unit in Phase 1 (later, with catch weight). Three-way match price comparison is per inventory unit: invoice line net amount / inventory quantity vs (V(I+q)-V(I))/q; price_tolerance_amount is per inventory unit, in the document currency.
9. (D6, sim 1) Proposed unit on a line, in order: partner_item unit (customer or supplier specific) -> supplier_item.order_uom_id (purchasing) -> product purchase_uom_id / sales_uom_id -> inventory unit. Barcode scans propose the barcode's unit; BOM lines set their own. The person may choose any allowed unit. The command response states the proposed unit's source.
10. (D7, U-03, sim 9) supplier_item gains order_uom_id (NOT NULL, default the product purchase unit at creation); minimum_order_quantity and order_multiple are in that unit. No supplier-specific factors: a different supplier pack is a separate product unit. Planning computes in inventory units, applies supplier minimum and multiple in the order unit (round up), converts exactly back; the purchase request line carries the order unit; a purchase order copied from a request keeps the request's unit unless the person changes it.
11. (D11, U-13, U-14) Packaging is a product unit. product_uom gains optional contains_uom_id + contains_quantity ("1 pallet contains 40 cartons") with a consistency check against the factors, an is_counting_unit flag (counting may be entered in several units, each converted) and an is_default_pick_unit flag. Quantity per handling unit type and packaging dimensions: after Phase 1 (state it).
12. (D13, U-09, U-20, sim 8) Price breaks are per unit (entered unit first, inventory-unit fallback as ADR-0045 r4; add the worked example). Promotion quantities are in the inventory unit and amount_per_unit is per inventory unit. Sales minimum/multiple and delivery rounding: after Phase 1, and then as check results, never silent rounding.
13. (D14, U-08, U-24, U-26) BOM component lines may use any allowed unit (stored with snapshot); optional issue_multiple rounds requirements up to whole cans/rolls. Production issue, receipt, confirmation backflush, transfer and count lines carry the line unit fields of decision 4. Align ADR-0033 r6 text with DDL (no default factor 1 hidden). quantity_per_value precision follows ADR-0006 quantity scale.
14. (D16-D17, U-10) Catch weight, batch-specific factors and cut-to-length remnants are after Phase 1. Phase 1 workaround stated: make weight (or length) the inventory unit and the piece a nominal product unit, flagged inexact; an item-level factor (decision 2) covers a configured item bought by length.
15. (D18, U-15, U-16) uom gains name (and translations through data_translation) and is_active; a core unit catalog catalogs/units.csv seeded from UN/ECE Rec 20 for the core kinds (piece, set, kg, g, t, m, cm, mm, m2, m3, l, ml, h, min, day, box/carton/pallet as company-addable patterns); decimal_places stays per unit (global). The Vietnamese package supplies Vietnamese unit names (DS-19 package data, unverified) used on prints and e-invoices: the e-invoice line prints the name of the line's own unit. E-invoice unit field length and the current decree text: flagged for DS-40 (open question).
16. (D19) uom_alias (company, alias text normalized, uom_id) for document intake by agents and imports; unknown unit text is never guessed: the line stays unresolved with check result unit_text_unresolved and a proposal to add an alias (change proposal, master_data_change).
17. (D20, sim 3, U-19) Query GetItemUnits (item) returns allowed units with factor, source, exactness, flags. Errors: uom_not_allowed for any line whose unit has no path (not only find_price); uom_retired; uom_kind_mismatch; serial_unit_not_whole; check results quantity_rounded, quantity_rounds_to_zero, unit_inexact, unit_text_unresolved. DB holds: positive factors, snapshot present on every line, inventory unit equals the product's (via check or trigger where feasible); the rest is command-level with invariants.
18. (U-17) New invariants (next free block, e.g. INV-116..) for: snapshot present and consistent; cumulative parts sum (decision 3); stock only in inventory unit; no line references a retired unit created after retirement; serial whole factors. New edge cases (EC-101..109 are free; use them): carton 24/1, bag 7/3 in three partial receipts, g<->kg global, item-level override, retired unit, unknown unit text from an e-invoice, invoice per kg against order in pieces, BOM in cans with issue multiple.
19. (U-18) ADR-0044 migration: units mapped first (phase 2 master data), legacy unit text through uom_alias; product_uom rows migrated with exactness classification; open lines migrated with their snapshot.
20. (U-05, U-25, U-27, U-28, U-29) uom_conversion: direct pairs only, inverse derived, immutable once used, is_active; product_uom gets a surrogate id (ADR-0006 r1) with UNIQUE (company_id, product_id, item_id, uom_id); uom.is_active documented; container volume/weight units from the supplier term (keep interim); quantity-based taxes and customs unit: seam reserved after Phase 1 (note in ADR-0041).
21. (sim 9) Purchase invoice agent D3 (ADR-0051 r21): order line selection compares inventory quantities and per-inventory-unit prices; an e-invoice line unit text resolves through uom_alias, else unit_text_unresolved.
Phase 1 scope as above; after Phase 1: catch weight, batch factors, order price unit, handling-unit quantities, packaging dimensions, sales multiples, quantity-based taxes.
