Note (author, 2026-11-21, session S2): on costing, NativeFlows follows how SAP does it. S/4HANA with the Material Ledger and actual costing is the reference for the costing data model and methods. D6 is not reopened: periodic weighted average with period-end actual costing corresponds to SAP's actual costing with a periodic unit price; the preliminary valuation during the period differs from SAP's and stays NativeFlows' way by the author's decision (exception 1 below). The SAP side was checked the same day against public SAP sources, with the evidence strength of each point (the section "Verification against SAP sources (2026-11-21)" of [reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md](reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md); the SAP comparison entry under "S2 closure (2026-11-21)" in [open-questions.md](open-questions.md)). After reading that comparison, the author decided six points the same day. Kept NativeFlows' way, as recorded deviations from SAP: (1) preliminary valuation: purchase receipts at document value and the preliminary-price chain ([ADR-0020](adr/0020-inventory-and-costing-model.md) rule 11 note); (4) the backdating window: any unlocked inventory period accepts postings ([ADR-0012](adr/0012-posting-periods-and-closing.md) rule 10 note); (6) sales returns at the original delivery line's issue cost, `issue_return` ([ADR-0020](adr/0020-inventory-and-costing-model.md) rule 5 note). Following SAP: (2) a late purchase cost (landed cost) is booked in the current period; chief architect's translation (interim, 2026-11-21): "the current period" is the period of the document's posting date, which must not be locked; a value-only `receipt_addition` on the item and valuation area in that period, with no recomputation of a locked period, no charge to past consumers and no forcing into the receipt's period; a document dated in an earlier period that is not locked makes that period stale like any backdated document ([ADR-0021](adr/0021-landed-cost.md) rule 6 note; [ADR-0020](adr/0020-inventory-and-costing-model.md) rule 15 note); (3) the exchange-rate difference between goods receipt and invoice is designed as SAP does; chief architect's translation (interim, 2026-11-21): for a stock item it is its own difference kind and enters the item's actual cost ([ADR-0047](adr/0047-commercial-documents-and-pricing.md) rule 13 note; ADR-0020 rule 12 note); (5) a reversed material document always takes the original line's value, a core rule ([ADR-0009](adr/0009-document-lifecycle.md) rule 20 note; ADR-0020 rule 7 note). Applied also in [open-questions.md](open-questions.md) (the entry on the cost of a sales return, 2026-11-21) and in the notes it names. The two DS-53 entries on standard cost were decided by the chief architect under the delegation note below, following this instruction. The remaining five points of the comparison (price control, delivery costs, the group-currency amount of costing lines, the actual cost component split, and the "not distributed" and "not allocated" differences) were settled by the chief architect on 2026-11-21 following SAP as verified, recorded in the notes headed "chief architect, 2026-11-21, under the author's delegation of that day; the author's instruction that costing follows SAP" at [ADR-0020](adr/0020-inventory-and-costing-model.md) rules 11, 12 and 13, [ADR-0018](adr/0018-product-and-item-model.md) rule 3, [ADR-0047](adr/0047-commercial-documents-and-pricing.md) rule 13, [ADR-0021](adr/0021-landed-cost.md) rule 12, [ADR-0011](adr/0011-multi-currency.md) rules 3 and 6 and [ADR-0072](adr/0072-costing-sheet-and-cost-component-structure.md) rule 4. Note (author, 2026-11-21, session S2; tax configuration): the author said, in translation: matters like tax rates are ideal objects of configuration: a global configuration, then configuration per category, then per product; the global default passes down; if the category differs, the category's is taken; if the product differs, the product's is taken; this solves tax rates that change over periods and countries that have different rates. The chief architect proposed configuring the item tax group, not the rate, at those three levels, with rates dated on the tax code, and the author agreed the same day, finding configuration by tax group more correct than assigning a tax directly to the product. Recorded by the chief architect: a rate change is one new dated `tax_rate` row and history never changes; the buyer side still decides the code through the matrix; the product's group is its own, else the nearest category's up the chain, else the company setting `tax.default_item_tax_group`, and none fails with `item_tax_group_missing`; each tax row records the group and its level, so later configuration changes never alter a document. A company in a country without a package may define its own codes, dated rates and matrix rows through a change proposal another person approves, counting as verified because the company states them, as the author decided for retention the same day; the core seeds no rate, and no company row shadows a package row ([ADR-0041](adr/0041-tax-engine.md) rules 2, 3, 4 and 7 notes; [ADR-0018](adr/0018-product-and-item-model.md) rules 3 and 16 notes).

Note (author, 2026-11-21, session S2; the two dimensions of tax): the author said, in translation: tax has two dimensions, one by product, as discussed, and one by type of transaction; for example goods taxed at 10% are sold domestically at 10% and exported at 0%; look at how the large systems do it. Decided by the chief architect under the author's delegation of that day, after a comparison of SAP S/4HANA, Dynamics 365, Oracle Fusion Tax, Odoo, NetSuite and SAP Business One ([reviews/2026-11-21-tax-determination-comparison.md](reviews/2026-11-21-tax-determination-comparison.md)), option B: the tax matrix keys on the two dimensions, (direction, tax treatment, item tax group, date) → tax codes, with rates dated on the code; the partner tax group is split into a partner tax status (what the party is, an input) and a tax treatment (what the transaction is); dated rules derive the treatment from the document's ship-from and ship-to countries, a zone flag, the partner's status and goods or service, the most specific rule winning; header and lines store the treatment and its source, and a manual change is flagged; five settings cover what the systems do differently ([ADR-0080](adr/0080-tax-treatment-determination.md)). Which cases are zero-rated in any country, and on what evidence, is not decided here; for Vietnam it waits for the Vietnam work.
