Note (author, 2026-11-21, session S2): on costing, NativeFlows follows how SAP does it. S/4HANA with the Material Ledger and actual costing is the reference for the costing data model and methods. D6 is not reopened: periodic weighted average with period-end actual costing corresponds to SAP's actual costing with a periodic unit price; the preliminary valuation during the period differs from SAP's and stays NativeFlows' way by the author's decision (exception 1 below). The SAP side was checked the same day against public SAP sources, with the evidence strength of each point (the section "Verification against SAP sources (2026-11-21)" of [reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md](reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md); the SAP comparison entry under "S2 closure (2026-11-21)" in [open-questions.md](open-questions.md)). After reading that comparison, the author decided six points the same day. Kept NativeFlows' way, as recorded deviations from SAP: (1) preliminary valuation: purchase receipts at document value and the preliminary-price chain ([ADR-0020](adr/0020-inventory-and-costing-model.md) rule 11 note); (4) the backdating window: any unlocked inventory period accepts postings ([ADR-0012](adr/0012-posting-periods-and-closing.md) rule 10 note); (6) sales returns at the original delivery line's issue cost, `issue_return` ([ADR-0020](adr/0020-inventory-and-costing-model.md) rule 5 note). Following SAP: (2) a late purchase cost (landed cost) is booked in the current period; chief architect's translation (interim, 2026-11-21): "the current period" is the period of the document's posting date, which must not be locked; a value-only `receipt_addition` on the item and valuation area in that period, with no recomputation of a locked period, no charge to past consumers and no forcing into the receipt's period; a document dated in an earlier period that is not locked makes that period stale like any backdated document ([ADR-0021](adr/0021-landed-cost.md) rule 6 note; [ADR-0020](adr/0020-inventory-and-costing-model.md) rule 15 note); (3) the exchange-rate difference between goods receipt and invoice is designed as SAP does; chief architect's translation (interim, 2026-11-21): for a stock item it is its own difference kind and enters the item's actual cost ([ADR-0047](adr/0047-commercial-documents-and-pricing.md) rule 13 note; ADR-0020 rule 12 note); (5) a reversed material document always takes the original line's value, a core rule ([ADR-0009](adr/0009-document-lifecycle.md) rule 20 note; ADR-0020 rule 7 note). Applied also in [open-questions.md](open-questions.md) (the entry on the cost of a sales return, 2026-11-21) and in the notes it names. The two DS-53 entries on standard cost were decided by the chief architect under the delegation note below, following this instruction. The remaining five points of the comparison (price control, delivery costs, the group-currency amount of costing lines, the actual cost component split, and the "not distributed" and "not allocated" differences) were settled by the chief architect on 2026-11-21 following SAP as verified, recorded in the notes headed "chief architect, 2026-11-21, under the author's delegation of that day; the author's instruction that costing follows SAP" at [ADR-0020](adr/0020-inventory-and-costing-model.md) rules 11, 12 and 13, [ADR-0018](adr/0018-product-and-item-model.md) rule 3, [ADR-0047](adr/0047-commercial-documents-and-pricing.md) rule 13, [ADR-0021](adr/0021-landed-cost.md) rule 12, [ADR-0011](adr/0011-multi-currency.md) rules 3 and 6 and [ADR-0072](adr/0072-costing-sheet-and-cost-component-structure.md) rule 4. The design freeze above stays: new costing design waits for its build step.
