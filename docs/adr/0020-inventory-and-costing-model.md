# ADR-0020: Inventory and costing model

Status: accepted (2026-11-14) under ADR-0005, reviewed by Claude Fable 5.2. Design item: DS-09 (ADR part for step 0; the period-end costing algorithm is specified for step 3). Related decisions: D5, D6, D10. Principles 2, 5, 6, 9. ADR-0004, ADR-0005, ADR-0006, ADR-0007, ADR-0008, ADR-0009, ADR-0010, ADR-0012, ADR-0014, ADR-0015, ADR-0016, ADR-0018.

## Decision

Quantities move in material documents; values move in journal entries; both belong to the same posting document and are written in the same transaction (spec [05](../spec/05-accounting-inventory-core.md)). Every material document line has a movement type from a core catalog that gives its direction, how its value is found, its role in costing and which transaction key offsets it; where these are accounting rules (returns, count differences) the catalog leaves them to a required regime setting. Stock quantity is kept per item, warehouse and lot or serial, and stock value per item and warehouse, both changed only through the stock lock helper, serialized per (item, warehouse), so stock never goes negative (Principle 9). Phase 1 uses periodic weighted average (D6): issues carry a deterministic preliminary price, and period-end actual costing computes each item's actual average per valuation area by an exact solve over all areas linked by transfers, then posts value-only adjustments with a derivation trace, never editing earlier lines. Goods in transit and goods sent are warehouses of their own kinds, attached to the valuation area of a physical warehouse, so transfers through them keep the issuing warehouse's average and their own accounts stay equal to the stock report. Lots and serials carry no cost. The schema leaves room for bills of materials (Phase 2) and catch weight (after Phase 1); bins and warehouse management are Phase 1 ([ADR-0023](0023-warehouse-management.md)).

### Rules

**Warehouses and valuation areas**

1. A `warehouse` belongs to one company: `code`, `name`, `kind` (`physical`; `transit` for goods the company owns on the way; `goods_sent` for goods delivered to customers or agents and still owned), `branch_id`, address, `is_active`, and for `transit` and `goods_sent`, `valuation_warehouse_id`: the physical warehouse whose valuation area it belongs to. The inventory key of a stock line follows the warehouse kind: `inventory_posting`, `inventory_in_transit_posting`, `inventory_goods_sent_posting` (ADR-0015). A `physical` warehouse may carry `processor_partner_id`; its stock uses the key `inventory_at_processor_posting` ([ADR-0037](0037-subcontracting.md) rule 1).

   Note ([ADR-0068](0068-sites-and-core-logistics-payment-master-data.md), 2026-11-21): a `physical` warehouse carries `site_id` (required). `transit` and `goods_sent` warehouses take the site of their `valuation_warehouse_id`. A warehouse's site changes only while it has no material document line (`warehouse_site_fixed`).
2. With `valuation_area_level` `company`, all warehouses of the company form one valuation area. With `warehouse`, each physical warehouse is a valuation area, together with the transit and goods-sent warehouses attached to it. A line records its area as `valuation_warehouse_id` (null for company level).

   Note ([ADR-0068](0068-sites-and-core-logistics-payment-master-data.md), 2026-11-21): the level `site` is added. All physical warehouses of a site, with their attached transit and goods-sent warehouses, form one valuation area. A line records its area as `valuation_site_id` at level `site`, as `valuation_warehouse_id` at level `warehouse`, and as neither at level `company`; at most one is set. Transfers inside one area are value-neutral, and at level `site` the exact solve of rule 12 runs over sites.

**Material documents and movement types**

3. A `material_document` belongs to one posting document (`document_id`, unique; ADR-0014 rule 2). Its lines (`material_document_line`) have `line_number`, `item_id`, `warehouse_id`, `movement_type_id`, `direction` (`in` or `out`, from the movement type, flipped when `is_reversal` is true), `is_reversal`, `quantity` (positive, in the item's inventory unit, ADR-0018 rule 11), `lot_id` or `serial_number_id` when the product is tracked, `company_amount` (the value moved), `valuation_warehouse_id`, `derivation_trace_id`, and the reserved `bin_id` and `catch_weight_quantity` (empty until after Phase 1). They are append-only, protected by triggers like journal rows (ADR-0014 rule 13); the journal line that posts each value refers to its material line. With warehouse management, `bin_id`, `stock_status` and `handling_unit_id` are active and filled where managed; moves inside a warehouse are warehouse movements, not material lines ([ADR-0023](0023-warehouse-management.md) rules 4, 7, 8). Lines also get nullable `cost_object_id` for Phase 2 ([ADR-0025](0025-later-phase-seams.md) rule 1). Lines also carry a reserved `owner_partner_id` ([ADR-0030](0030-commercial-seams-and-imports.md) rule 8). `bin_id` is NOT NULL (a warehouse that is not bin-managed uses its default bin), and lines carry `posting_date` ([ADR-0031](0031-design-simplifications.md) rules 8, 12).

   Note ([ADR-0056](0056-multiple-units-of-measure.md), 2026-11-15): a material line's `quantity` is its source document line's `inventory_quantity`; the entered unit and its snapshot stay on the document line (rules 1, 9).

   Note ([ADR-0068](0068-sites-and-core-logistics-payment-master-data.md), 2026-11-21): lines also carry `valuation_site_id`, set by the stock lock helper with `valuation_warehouse_id` (INV-810).
4. The movement type catalog is `docs/catalogs/movement-types.csv` (core catalog, uuid5 IDs, versioned like the system chart, 0.x until the step 0 gate; ADR-0010 rule 9). Each type declares `code`, `name`, `direction`, `value_source` (`document`: from the document; `preliminary_price`; `issuing_value`: the value that left, for the receiving side of a transfer; `regime`: rule 5), `costing_role` (`receipt`: enters the period's receipts, negative when the direction is out; `revalued_issue`: valued at the preliminary price and revalued at period end; `transfer`; `regime`), `offset_transaction_key` (empty for transfers), `needs_reference` and its nearest SAP analog. Version 0.1.0 has 13 types (20 with the production types of [ADR-0035](0035-production-costing.md) rule 19, which add the costing roles `production_input` and `production_output`): purchase receipt and return, sales issue and return receipt, transfer out and in, stock count shortage and surplus, internal consumption, other receipt, other issue, scrap and opening balance. Moving goods into or out of transit or goods-sent warehouses is a transfer; selling goods sent is a sales issue from the goods-sent warehouse. This ADR adds the keys `other_receipt_offset`, `other_issue_offset` and `scrap` to the transaction key catalog. Production types are reserved for Phase 2; now Phase 1, named in [ADR-0034](0034-production-orders-and-shop-floor.md) (DS-53 adds the rows).

   Note: the counts above are those of version 0.1.0 when this rule was written. The catalog has since gained `free_goods_issue` ([ADR-0047](0047-commercial-documents-and-pricing.md) rule 17), so it holds 21 types at this note; [movement-types.csv](../catalogs/movement-types.csv) is the authority for the count.

   Note ([ADR-0070](0070-quality-management-core.md), 2026-11-21): the catalog gains `quality_sample` (out, preliminary price, offset `consumption`), used by `internal_consumption` lines for samples destroyed by testing (ADR-0070 rule 15).

   Note (OQ-150, chief architect, 2026-11-21): the costing roles gain `issue_return`: a negative issue that names the original issue line it returns (for a sales return receipt, the delivery's material line, through `return_from_customer_line.delivery_line_id`; the movement type has `needs_reference` true). Its value source stays `document`. No catalog type carries it fixed; a regime selects it for `sales_return_receipt` (rule 5 note). How it takes part in costing: rule 12 note.
5. Value source and costing role of `purchase_return`, `sales_return_receipt`, `stock_count_shortage` and `stock_count_surplus` are accounting rules, so the catalog marks them `regime` and the core reads them only from the required regime settings `movement_type.<code>.value_source` and `movement_type.<code>.costing_role` (ADR-0016 rule 10): a posting without them fails with `regime_configuration_missing`, and an `unverified` one is refused in production (ADR-0016 rule 12). The Vietnam package (DS-19) seeds them with basis `unverified` and the interim values in the catalog's `meaning` column, until the author answers the open questions (sales return cost; purchase return value; count difference valuation). `by_product_receipt` is valued by the regime too ([ADR-0035](0035-production-costing.md) rule 13). TT99 seeds: [ADR-0039](0039-vietnam-package-tt99.md) rule 9.

The catalog's interim seed for `purchase_return` is value source `document` with costing role `receipt`, a negative receipt. For a return of goods received against a purchase order line, the document value is the GR/IR debit of the positions the return debits, round(V(R)) − round(V(R − q)) at the line's latest received positions ([ADR-0047](0047-commercial-documents-and-pricing.md) rule 13 and its note; [ADR-0056](0056-multiple-units-of-measure.md) rule 10 note). While the order line has one GR/IR price it equals the value of the receipt the return names; after a change order it may not. A regime that sets another value source, such as the current average, posts the difference from the GR/IR debit as a price difference on the return ([ADR-0047](0047-commercial-documents-and-pricing.md) rule 13). Whether a return carries landed cost allocated to its receipt stays the open question of [ADR-0021](0021-landed-cost.md) rule 3. Catalog meaning in [movement-types.csv](../catalogs/movement-types.csv); lab check `t17_uom.py` U71.

The optional key `movement_type.purchase_return.include_landed_cost` (core default true) makes a return carry its receipt's share of allocated landed cost. Each return stamps the options in force (rule 24).

6. A transfer posts both stock lines at the issuing value, and each stock line always has its journal line (credit on the issuing warehouse, debit on the receiving one, carrying their `warehouse_id`), even when both determine the same operational account, so material line values equal their journal lines, inventory value per warehouse as a view over `journal_line` (ADR-0014 rule 17) equals `stock_value`, and every inventory account equals the stock value of its warehouses. A transfer between valuation areas moves at the issuing area's average: preliminary during the period, actual after costing ([08](../spec/08-l10n-vn.md#costing)).
7. Reversible stock document types are transfers, stock counts, internal consumption, other receipts and issues, scrap and opening balances, as internal documents (ADR-0009 rule 19); goods receipts and deliveries are corrected by return documents (ADR-0009 rule 23), with their flags set by DS-22. A reversal repeats the lines with `is_reversal` true and the opposite direction; each line keeps its type's costing role with the opposite sign (a negative receipt, a negative issue), and its value follows the flagged regime setting for the value of a reversed material document (ADR-0009 rule 20; interim: the original line's value). It is refused when the stock it returns has been consumed, which is the check of rule 10 (ADR-0009 rule 18). Reversal with bins: [ADR-0023](0023-warehouse-management.md) rule 9. Aligned by [ADR-0027](0027-cross-cutting-ownership.md) rule 13: `goods_receipt` and `delivery` follow ADR-0009 rule 19's flagged interim (reversible while unreferenced), with their flags set by the DS-09 stock documents deliverable.

    Note ([ADR-0075](0075-stock-documents.md), 2026-11-21): the typed headers and lines of every stock document type of this rule are defined: the stock transfer, the stock count, scrap and internal consumption (ADR-0075 rules 1 to 6), other receipts and other issues (rules 11, 12) and the opening balance (rule 13), all reversible as this rule says; the non-posting transfer order is rule 14.

    Note (OQ-150, 2026-11-21): where a return document is reversed, the reversal of an `issue_return` line is an `issue_return` line of the opposite sign that names the same original line, valued as this rule says (interim: the reversed line's value). The solve treats it with the line it reverses (rule 12 note): revalued with the original when the original is in the period being costed, otherwise kept at its value, so the two net to zero.

   Note (the author, 2026-11-21): the value of a reversed material document is a core rule, no longer a flagged regime setting: a reversal line always takes the original line's value. Checked against SAP sources (point C3 of the verification section of [reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md](../reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md)): SAP reverses a goods receipt at the original document's value only when the reversal refers to that one receipt; without the reference it takes the average receipt value of the order item, and after the invoice the invoice price. A NativeFlows reversal is always the `Reverse` of one document and repeats that document's lines, and a document that another document refers to (an invoice, a landed cost) cannot be reversed ([ADR-0009](0009-document-lifecycle.md) rule 19), so "the original line's value" is SAP's referenced case and the other two cases do not arise. SAP's value for reversals of issues and transfers was not verified. This holds for the reversal of an `issue_return` line too (OQ-150 note above). The checks still apply to a reversal: no negative quantity (rule 10), and the degenerate cases of rule 13 for a value left at zero quantity or a negative value. [ADR-0009](0009-document-lifecycle.md) rule 20 note of this date. `settings.csv` never had a row for it; the Vietnam package's `reversal.material_document_value` goes in its rework.

**Stock balances and locking**

8. `stock_quantity` holds quantity per (company, item, warehouse, lot or serial, bin reserved); `stock_value` holds value per (company, item, warehouse). The stock valuation report sums `stock_value` per item, valuation area and inventory account; the inventory-control accounts equal it (DS-16). Both tables are written only through the stock lock helper in the posting transaction and read only through its functions and views (ADR-0008 rule 10); no other SQL or trigger writes them. Note: the key gains bin, `stock_status` and handling unit, and warehouse movements also change it ([ADR-0023](0023-warehouse-management.md) rules 4, 8). The quantity key gains `owner_partner_id`, null for own stock; non-owned stock has no value ([ADR-0030](0030-commercial-seams-and-imports.md) rule 8). Every warehouse has a default bin, so the bin is never null ([ADR-0031](0031-design-simplifications.md) rules 7, 8).

    Note (author's delegation, 2026-11-21; decided by the chief architect): the key of `stock_value` gains a reserved nullable `valuation_type` (unique key with NULLS NOT DISTINCT), never set in Phase 1; see the note at rule 17. A split-valued item is lot-tracked, and each lot carries one valuation type, so the quantity key (`stock_quantity`) keeps its lot axis and does not change.
9. The helper locks, for every (item, warehouse) a document touches, its `stock_value` row (created on first use), in ascending order of item and warehouse IDs, before any numbering lock; quantity rows of lots and serials are reached only under that lock. Value-only inventory journal lines (landed cost added to stock, price differences to stock, costing adjustments) carry item and warehouse (ADR-0014 rule 10) and go through the same helper, which updates `stock_value`; ADR-0010 rule 5 counts them as postings through the inventory module. Warehouse movements and reservations use the same helper; the full lock order is [ADR-0023](0023-warehouse-management.md) rule 10.
10. No stock quantity may be negative, and a serial number never has more than one unit on hand. Under the helper's lock, a posting is rejected with `negative_stock` if, for any (item, warehouse, lot or serial) it touches, the closing quantity of its posting date or of any later date would fall below zero; quantities within one day are not ordered (ADR-0007 rule 10). Whether this end-of-day rule is the right time point for backdated documents is the existing open question; it is the interim rule, flagged. Note: this dated check stays per (item, warehouse, lot or serial); bins, statuses and handling units are checked on their current balance ([ADR-0023](0023-warehouse-management.md) rule 5). The check reads `material_document_line.posting_date` through its index, per key on the document's net change, by the algorithm of [ADR-0031](0031-design-simplifications.md) rule 14.

    Note (S2 open questions, 2026-11-21): the rule is the answer, no longer flagged: a backdated posting is rejected when any closing quantity from its posting date onward would be negative, per (item, warehouse, lot or serial).

**Price control and costing**

11. Price control is `periodic_average` (ADR-0018 rule 3; D6). `preliminary_price` rows hold, per (item, valuation area, posting period), the price issues use. When period N−1 has an actual average for the item and area, the row for N is that average. Otherwise the price is the unit value of the latest movement with costing role `receipt` or a `transfer_in`, in that area, on or before the line's posting date, ordered by posting date, document type code, document number and `line_number`; if the area has none, the item's latest such price in any area of the company; if there is none at all, a line that needs the preliminary price (an issue, or a count surplus valued at it) is rejected with `preliminary_price_missing`, naming the item, until a receipt exists or a human sets a price with `UpdatePreliminaryPrice` (`human_only`, audited, since under rule 13 it can become the final cost). Such a line's value is `round_amount(quantity × preliminary price)` (ADR-0006 rule 18). The fallback also reads receipts with costing role `production_output`; an item without history takes its released cost estimate's unit cost ([ADR-0035](0035-production-costing.md) rule 4). At migration, the opening value per item and valuation area divided by its quantity is the first preliminary price ([ADR-0044](0044-data-migration-and-export.md) rule 9).

    Note: "its released cost estimate" is the `cost_estimate` of [ADR-0035](0035-production-costing.md) rule 14, selected as [ADR-0046](0046-product-configuration.md) rule 14's note says: the latest released one for the item with costing date on or before the line's posting date, preferring the version whose production warehouse is in the line's valuation area; price basis ADR-0035 rule 14's default (flagged).

    Note (UoM simulation review, 2026-11-15): the preliminary price of a purchased item before its first receipt. When the steps above find no price (no actual average of N−1 and no receipt, in the area or in the company), an item that is not produced takes the first of:
    1. its supplier purchase list price: `find_price` on the purchase list of the item's preferred `supplier_item` supplier (that supplier profile's purchase list, else the company's default purchase list), or on the company's default purchase list when the item has no preferred supplier item, for one inventory unit in the inventory unit, on the line's posting date, in company currency at the applied rate of that date. Only entries in the inventory unit qualify, since `find_price` never converts another unit's price down ([ADR-0056](0056-multiple-units-of-measure.md) rule 16): a list priced only per carton gives no price here;
    2. else its last purchase invoice price: the net amount after the document discount divided by the inventory quantity ([ADR-0056](0056-multiple-units-of-measure.md) rule 12) of the item's latest posted purchase invoice line with a quantity in the company, on or before the posting date (by posting date, document number and `line_number`), in company currency at that invoice's applied rate;
    3. else a manual preliminary price, set with `UpdatePreliminaryPrice`;
    4. else a line that needs a price is rejected with `preliminary_price_missing`, as above.

    `preliminary_price.price_source` records `purchase_list` or `last_purchase_invoice` for the first two (DS-22-09-42), with a derivation trace naming the list entry or the invoice line. A produced item, one with an active production version of its own or of its configurable product ([ADR-0033](0033-production-master-data.md) rule 13), keeps its chain: receipts with costing role `production_output`, then its released cost estimate's unit cost (the note above; [ADR-0035](0035-production-costing.md) rule 4), then a manual price, else `preliminary_price_missing`; a purchase list or invoice never prices it. Lab check `t17_uom.py` U72.

    Note ([ADR-0068](0068-sites-and-core-logistics-payment-master-data.md), 2026-11-21): `preliminary_price` is keyed by the area's pair (`valuation_site_id`, `valuation_warehouse_id`). At level `site`, "the area" in the fallbacks is the site.

    Note (the author, 2026-11-21): kept as written: purchase receipts post at document value, and issues at the preliminary price of this rule, which the fallbacks can change inside a period. This is a recorded deviation from SAP, where every movement of the period is valued at one period price and the differences are collected per material. Checked against SAP sources only in part (point A1 of the verification section of [reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md](../reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md)): SAP's actual costing requires standard-price control, so its period price is the standard price; the in-period valuation of each movement was not checked separately.

    Note (chief architect, 2026-11-21; the author's instruction that costing follows SAP): price control, settled following SAP as verified (point A1 of the verification section of [reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md](../reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md)). In SAP, actual costing works only with standard-price control, set per material and valuation area (and per valuation type under split valuation); moving average has no periodic actual price. `periodic_average` is that model: SAP's standard price plus actual costing, where this rule's preliminary price plays the standard price of the period. Phase 1 keeps the single value on the product ([ADR-0018](0018-product-and-item-model.md) rules 3, 18). The ADR written after Phase 1 for the standard-price option (DS-53 entries in [open-questions.md](../open-questions.md)) makes it the preliminary price of this method, not a separate method, and keys price control by item and valuation area, with the reserved `valuation_type` (rule 17 notes). A moving-average option, if ever added, stays outside periodic actual costing: its items are not revalued by rule 12. Seam check: no posted row stores price control (no column on `material_document_line`, `stock_value`, `journal_line` or `preliminary_price`; only `product.price_control` in DS-20), and `preliminary_price` is already keyed by item, valuation area and period, the key a standard price of the period needs (a `price_source` value is added then). Moving the key later changes master data only, not posted data, so no seam is added now.
12. Period-end actual costing is a sequential close task per company and period (ADR-0012 rules 13, 16). For each item it computes the actual average of every valuation area from the opening value and quantity plus the period's receipts, including value-only additions classed by the `inventory_value_role` their document type declares in the registry (`receipt_addition` for landed cost and price differences to stock; `costing_adjustment` for costing adjustment documents, which do not count; ADR-0008 rule 1), solving the strongly connected components of the areas linked by the period's flows in topological order, each as one exact linear system in rational arithmetic ([ADR-0025](0025-later-phase-seams.md) rule 6), so the result is the same on every run and equal to the naive recomputation. It posts value-only adjustment documents (one per company and period, or split by item range when large) that revalue revalued issues and transfers to actual averages, with each movement type's offset key, and a derivation trace per item and area (Principle 6). BOM levels join the system in Phase 2 ([05](../spec/05-accounting-inventory-core.md)); now Phase 1, designed in DS-53 ([ADR-0032](0032-phase-1-scope-with-production.md)). Production cost-object nodes, pools and WIP join this solve ([ADR-0035](0035-production-costing.md) rule 6).

    Note (OQ-150, chief architect, 2026-11-21): a line with costing role `issue_return` (rule 4 note) takes part in this solve as follows. (a) When the original issue line is in the period being costed, in the same valuation area, the return is revalued together with it at the area's actual average, so the pair nets to an issue of the net quantity. (b) When the original line is in an earlier period, the return keeps the original line's final unit cost after that period's costing. It is counted neither in the period's receipts nor among the revalued issues; the difference between its value and the period's actual average times its quantity stays in the area's closing value and enters the next period through the opening value, with no posting. The rounding residual of rule 13 is measured after setting that difference aside. When the closing quantity is zero, the period's issues stay at the actual average, and the set-aside difference, with any rounding, is posted as the rule 13 residual (`inventory_revaluation_offset` key, with a derivation trace); the presence of a return never moves an area's issues to the preliminary price. Returned units issued again in the same period are issues like any other, valued at the period's actual average; the return's difference stays in the closing value, or goes to the rule 13 residual when the closing quantity is zero. Rule 13's other triggers apply as written: an area with no opening quantity and no receipts, or whose average would be negative, values its issues at the preliminary price and posts the residual (S2 schema review, findings S7 and S8). A closing value that would be negative only because of returns kept at their own value is treated like a closing quantity of zero: the issues stay at the actual average, the kept differences are posted as the rule 13 residual, and the area closes at the actual average times its closing quantity (S2 schema review round 2, R2-S1). (c) When the earlier period's costing changes the original line's value, on its first run or a rerun (rule 14), the return follows by a value-only adjustment posted by the costing of the return's own period, dated in that period: that period's sequential task goes back to `pending` when the earlier one runs again (ADR-0012 rule 16). In no case does a return enter an average, so the linear system of this rule is unchanged and stays exact. A return received into another valuation area than its original's is valued at the original line's cost and stays outside the receiving area's average, as in (b), also when the original is in the same period; its value then follows the original's final cost through the adjustment of (c). A partial return takes the cumulative share of the original line's value V: after r of the line's quantity Q has been returned, a return of q takes round(V × (r + q) / Q) − round(V × r / Q), so the returns of a whole line total exactly V. A sales return receipt line always has an original: goods delivered before the migration cut-over come back as `other_receipt` ([ADR-0047](0047-commercial-documents-and-pricing.md) rule 8) and `return_from_customer_line.delivery_line_id` is NOT NULL, so no preliminary-price value is defined for `issue_return` lines. Lab: `costing_solver.py` `check_issue_returns`, run by `t17_uom.py` U73 to U88, checks cases (a), (b), the next-period absorption, a zero closing quantity, a rerun, a return into another area, cumulative partial returns, the three rule 13 triggers and returned units issued again against hand-computed values and against `independent_values`, a recomputation with its own code path; the comparison of the two posting paths shows that the result does not depend on the order of posting.

    Note (the author, 2026-11-21): the exchange-rate difference between goods receipt and purchase invoice is designed as SAP does, where it is kept apart from price differences and is part of a stock item's actual cost. Checked against SAP sources (point C2 of the verification section of [reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md](../reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md)): this is SAP's default; SAP also lets a company keep such differences out of inventory, which NativeFlows offers by the company setting `costing.exchange_rate_difference_to_stock` ([ADR-0047](0047-commercial-documents-and-pricing.md) rule 13 note of the chief architect, 2026-11-21). Lines without stock post it to the exchange-rate difference accounts, as before ([ADR-0047](0047-commercial-documents-and-pricing.md) rule 13 note; [ADR-0011](0011-multi-currency.md) rule 6 note; [ADR-0021](0021-landed-cost.md) rule 12 note).

    Note (chief architect, 2026-11-21; the author's instruction that costing follows SAP): the group-currency amount of stock and costing lines, settled following SAP as verified (point A3 of the verification section of [reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md](../reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md): every movement is translated at the rate of its own posting date, and prices and stock values are computed separately in each currency). Interim; the step 3 costing specification confirms the details.
    - **Movements.** Every journal line of a stock movement or a value-only inventory line has its group amount by [ADR-0011](0011-multi-currency.md) rules 2 and 3: translated at posting from its transaction amount with the document's applied group rate, the rate of its own posting date. A movement's group amount is that of the inventory journal line that posts it (rule 6). `material_document_line` and `stock_value` stay in company currency (ADR-0011 rule 6); the group value of an item and warehouse is the sum of its inventory journal lines' group amounts ([ADR-0014](0014-universal-journal.md) rule 17).
    - **Period end.** The solve of this rule runs once per carried currency. In group currency each area's actual average is computed from the group amounts of its opening value and receipts, through the same flows (transfers, orders, pools, WIP), and each revalued line's group adjustment is that average times its quantity less the line's group amount. The company-currency result is never retranslated. The coefficients of the linear system are quantities and shares, so both currencies share one system and differ only in its right-hand side: the second currency costs one more right-hand side per component, not a second graph. A group average may rise while the company average falls.
    - **Posting.** A costing adjustment line carries the company and group adjustments of the same item, warehouse and offset key; when they have opposite signs, or the company adjustment is zero, the group part is a separate group-only line (transaction and company amount 0, ADR-0011 rule 2), so the sign rule of [ADR-0014](0014-universal-journal.md) holds. Rule 13 applies per currency: its residuals, rounding included, post in each currency, the group part as a group-only line; which degenerate case applies is decided on quantities and in company currency, and the group currency follows it. An `issue_return` line keeps its original line's final group cost as it keeps the company cost.
    - **Currencies.** Phase 1 carries one currency or two: company currency always, and the installation's group currency when it is set (ADR-0011 rule 6). A third valuation (profit center) is not designed. Costing adjustment lines take their group amount from the group-currency solve, an exception to ADR-0011 rule 3's "group amounts are always converted from the transaction amount" (ADR-0011 rule 3 note of this date).
    - **Lab.** `costing_solver.py` `check_group_currency`, run by `t17_uom.py` U89: on two areas linked by a transfer, with rates that move during the period, the group-currency averages from the solve equal an independent recomputation on group amounts (the naive iteration), group value is conserved, and they differ from the company result retranslated at the period-end rate; no single rate maps both areas' company averages to their group averages.

    Note (chief architect, 2026-11-21; the author's instruction that costing follows SAP): the actual cost component split, settled following SAP as verified (point A4 of the verification section of [reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md](../reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md): the split of the standard cost estimate carried through actual costing across production levels, activated at the start of a period and never built for periods already posted). Phase 1 keeps the seam and builds no split through stock: production orders already keep actual cost per posting component, and per detailed component as a read model ([ADR-0072](0072-costing-sheet-and-cost-component-structure.md) rule 4). The feature, when built, carries values per posting component along the same flows of this rule's solve; the coefficients are the same, so each component is one more right-hand side, and the components of a node sum exactly to its value. It stores its result as a read model per item, valuation area, period and component beside the postings, which it never changes. As in SAP it starts at a period start: the opening split of a purchased item is all `material`; that of a produced item follows the components of its released cost estimate (interim). Why the seam is enough: nothing posted changes, and a start at a period start needs no history, so leaving it out of Phase 1 makes no later migration; building it now adds a stored figure per item and area that no Phase 1 report reads. How SAP assigns purchase price differences to components was not found; interim: receipt additions go to `material`.

Degenerate cases follow one rule: when an item's area has no receipts and no opening quantity, when its closing quantity is zero but value remains, when its closing value or its average would be negative, or when a component's system has no unique solution (e.g. goods transferred back and forth within a day; other components are solved normally), the area keeps the preliminary price for its issues, and any residual value is posted per warehouse as its own line with the `inventory_revaluation_offset` key and a derivation trace (interim, flagged: where such residuals go is an accounting rule; the regime may map the key). Rounding residuals of the revaluation are posted the same way (ADR-0006 rule 21). The formulas and the adjustment document layout are specified for step 3.

    Note (S2 schema review, 2026-11-21): one exception, in the rule 12 OQ-150 note: in an area holding `issue_return` lines kept at their own value, a closing quantity of zero, or a closing value negative only because of those lines, leaves the issues at the actual average; the kept difference is posted as this rule's residual.

    Note (chief architect, 2026-11-21; the author's instruction that costing follows SAP): SAP's "not distributed" and "not allocated" differences mapped to NativeFlows (points A5a and A5b of the verification section of [reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md](../reviews/2026-11-21-costing-sap-comparison.md)). No new mechanism is needed.
    - **"Not distributed"**: differences a stock-coverage check keeps from consumption and ending stock, or that would give a negative price; in SAP they stay on the price difference account. Under the exact solve of rule 12 every receipt and receipt addition of an area enters its average and every movement is valued at it; stock never goes negative (rule 10), so an area's quantities always balance and a coverage gap cannot come from quantities. What remains are this rule's cases: value left at a zero closing quantity, a negative average or closing value, a system without a unique solution, and the kept `issue_return` differences of the rule 12 note. Their residual posts to `inventory_revaluation_offset` and stays there, as SAP's stays on the price difference account: this rule's residual is the equivalent. SAP's negative stock as a cause was not verified, and it cannot occur here.
    - **"Not allocated"**: single-level consumption differences not passed up to the higher level. Of SAP's causes, cycles are solved exactly by rule 12, so none is left; rounding is this rule's rounding residual; no receipt of the higher-level product in the period means the consumption stays on the order as work in process and moves on with it ([ADR-0035](0035-production-costing.md) rule 9), or, on a technically complete order, goes to cost of goods sold as `production_residual_cost` (ADR-0035 rule 10). Consumption that could not be revalued does not arise, since every consumer is revalued (rule 12).
    - A report of these amounts per item and area reads the derivation traces of the residual, WIP and residual-cost postings; nothing is stored for it.

## Context

DS-09's ADR part is needed by ADR-0014 (material document link), ADR-0015 (inventory keys), DS-11 (landed cost) and DS-16 (stock invariants); the algorithm by step 3. Constraints:

- Material and accounting documents written together; movement types as configuration deciding quantity and value updates and transaction keys; price control per item with a preliminary price; period-end actual costing solving warehouses together (BOM levels in Phase 2; now Phase 1, [ADR-0032](0032-phase-1-scope-with-production.md)); landed cost rules ([05](../spec/05-accounting-inventory-core.md)). Periodic weighted average per warehouse or company; transfers at the issuing warehouse's average ([08](../spec/08-l10n-vn.md#costing)).
- Lots and serials without cost; stock and cost in the stock unit; backdated documents re-cost unlocked periods with adjustment documents ([07](../spec/07-shared-foundation.md)). Stock posting serialized per (item, warehouse) (Principle 9). No negative stock and inventory accounts equal to the valuation report are gate conditions ([12](../spec/12-testing.md); step 3).
- Open questions: negative-stock time point, sales return cost, reversed material document value, costing scope change, where the existing costing engine lives.
- SAP practice (D5): material documents with movement types (101, 122, 301, 601, 651, 701/702 …), valuation area, stock in transit as a stock type, material ledger periodic unit price with multilevel settlement. ADR-0005: the mechanism is the agent's, the accounting rules the author's.

## Options considered

| Option | For | Against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transit and goods sent as warehouse kinds attached to a physical warehouse's area (chosen) | One stock model; their accounts reconcile; transfers keep the issuing average | A setting per transit warehouse |
| Stock types or special stock flags (SAP); later replaced by the stock status axis of [ADR-0023](0023-warehouse-management.md) | Fewer warehouses | A second axis in every stock query and key |
| Value per (item, valuation area) only | One value row | Cannot reconcile accounts that differ by warehouse kind |
| Exact rational solve (chosen) | Deterministic; equals the naive recomputation | Larger systems for many linked areas |
| Iteration to a tolerance | Simple | Results depend on the tolerance; `plan_hash` and references may differ |
| Fixed value sources for returns and counts in the core | Simple | Decides accounting rules reserved to the author |
| Skip journal lines for same-account transfers | Fewer lines | Two posting shapes; per-warehouse value from the journal no longer equals stock value |
